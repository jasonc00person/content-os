# Jason's Voice DNA

The voice rulebook for every script. It was rebuilt on 2026-09-22 from Jason's **8 latest videos**, word for word: the 5 newest IG reels (2026-06-10 to 07-01) and the 3 newest YouTube long-forms (2026-08-07 to 09-15). The transcripts are in `transcripts/own/latest_2026-09-22/`, and they're also corpus entries in `voice-corpus/`. IG counts are likes because logged-out pulls don't return plays. YT counts are views. The sample is thin (8 entries), so treat anything that shows up only once as a lean, not a law. The previous rulebook, built from the 42 top videos of all time, is at `voice-dna.previous.md`.

Transcription artifacts are NOT voice. Whisper and YouTube captions write "ChatGBT", "Clawed", "quad skill", "Habata", "Higgs Field" and "school". In scripts, write GPT, Claude, Claude skill, Hbada, Higgsfield and Skool.

## Speech Patterns & Signature Phrases

### Openers

- **"This video is the demo" (3 of 8, the dominant shape right now).** The thing you're watching is the proof. "This video that you're watching right now was edited by Claude entirely from end to end." (2,869 likes, the top IG) / "This video was edited by Claude in one shot." (57.9K) / "I gave ChatGBT Astro 6 one single prompt and this entire video is what I got back." (7.6K)
- **"I just replaced $X/mo of [people] with one [tool]" (1 of 5 IG).** It carries an exact dollar figure and a setup time. "I just replaced my two thousand dollar a month content research team with one single quad skill and it only took me 30 minutes to set up." (595 likes)
- **A question aimed at the hype (2 of 5 IG).** He names the trend everyone is pushing and asks whether it's real. "Building a second brain with AI is this actually useful or is it just LARP?" (1,179 likes) / "Clawed this, clawed that, but where does AI actually belong in the content creation process?" (441 likes)
- **"Behold" plus a comic deflation (top YT, 95.9K).** Big claim, then a joke, then "No, but seriously guys." "Behold, Claude can now take full control over DaVinci Resolve... This is big news for the unemployed as we now have more time to scroll Instagram reels while Claude does all of our work for us."
- **"That's right" confirms the hook (3 of 8).** "That's right. I didn't have to sit there and babysit..." / "That's right, they put AI in the chair..."
- **Sentence two proves it against the skeptic (all 3 YT).** "Now, the footage that you're watching is of course actually me. It's not AI generated, but every single cut..." / "I even recorded the entire process... So, for you skeptics out there, you can literally watch that entire video." He points to a livestream or a recording as receipts in 2 of 3 YT.

### Filler / Transition Words

- **The two registers split hard.** IG is tight and scripted: 1 to 3 uses of "like" per reel, zero "you know", one "right?". YT is loose and conversational: 33 to 56 uses of "like" per video, "you know" 29 times, the tag question "right?" 18 times. Write IG clean and let YT breathe.
- **"So," starts screen-share beats (90 times across YT).** "So, basically, it's a Claude code project with a bunch of files, right?" / "All right, guys. So, I'm going to cover everything..."
- **"Now," pivots beats (41 times across YT).** "Now, the real reason that Astra is able to edit this video so well is..." / "Now, a lot of people are probably wondering about the cost."
- **"Actually" is the credibility word (41 uses, 6 of 8 videos).** "actually postable", "actually useful", "actually good", "actually me". Use it where the claim needs to sound tested, not hyped.
- **Demo scaffolds.** "Here's how it works." / "Let me show you exactly what this entire automation does." / "First up... Next... Now here's the part where it gets crazy." (IG demo) / "Let's start off with my favorite part... All right, let's talk about the bad news." (review)
- **"Blah blah blah" skips a boring list (3 times in YT).** "assets, docs, packaging, presets, blah blah blah."
- **"Basically" explains architecture, "obviously" covers prerequisites (7 each, YT only).**

### Closers

- **IG uses a comment keyword (4 of 5).** One caps word tied to the topic: EDIT, LARP, BUILD, X7. "just comment build down below and I'll send you the full video." In 3 of those 4 the reel is a trailer, and the keyword delivers the full YouTube video.
- **YT pitches the Skool 2 or 3 times per video (all 3).** He pitches once early as "skip the tutorial", sometimes again mid-roll, then at the close. "join my school at the first link down in the description" ("first link" 4 times). The pitch stack is: you get the files and you own them ("you just get the files", no SaaS), a setup guide, group calls twice a week (Tuesday and Thursday), daily Loom replies, then everything else in the classroom. Pull the offer details from `backbone/offer.md`. Never hardcode a price.
- **An honest caveat comes right before the pitch (2 of 3 YT).** "Fair warning, it only is really good at like talking head educational videos."
- **Free path vs. paid path (2 of 3 YT).** There's a free prompt in the description for people who want to build it themselves, and the Skool folder for people who value their time. "If you're someone who values your time..."
- **Sign-off (3 of 3 YT):** "I hope you guys found this video valuable. I'll see you in the next one. Peace." Variants are "Peace out." and "happy editing."

### Energy Markers

- **Slang in rotation:** dialed in (4), put you on game (2), cracked, cook, clutch, bro (6, most of them in the Astra rant), dude, super duper, LARP, AI slop (3), "yo, put me in this thumbnail."
- **Comic deflation and self-aware jokes (3 of 8).** "big news for the unemployed" / on the chair's ball fan: "it's important to keep the boys cool" / on the massage rollers: "Kind of reminds me to strain my back."
- **Live payoff exclamations when a result lands.** "Yo, let's go. Okay, it did it. It did it. It did it." / "Boom."
- **Doubling for emphasis.** "Huge, huge time save." / "It looks bad, bro. It looks bad."
- **"I promise" as reassurance (5, YT).** "I promise it's going to save you a ton of hours."
- **Light profanity only.** One "shitty" and one "swamp ass" across 8 videos. It's seasoning, not the base.

## Voice Characteristics

- **Anti-guru, anti-LARP (4 of 8).** "Here is the harsh reality that a lot of these AI gurus aren't telling you about." He's the guy who actually built it and tested it: he skipped the guru version and "went straight to the source" (the Karpathy gist).
- **An honest reviewer who will trash the thing.** "Astra sucks." / "let's talk about the bad news. I wouldn't really call it AI assisted." Verdicts come with a reason and he picks a side (Claude over OpenAI, on quality and on values).
- **Exact numbers, never rounded.** 2 hours 42 minutes, 740k context, $3,600 of credits, $30 a month vs. $295 lifetime, twice a week.
- **Hedges honestly about what he hasn't tested.** "I haven't tested it, but I'm pretty sure it will work with the MCP." / "I think Sonnet 5 will work, but you might run into limits."
- **Beginner reassurance before a tutorial (3 of 8).** "even if you've never coded or you've never edited a video in your life, don't worry, I'm gonna make this super duper simple."
- **Talks to "you guys" (41 times in YT, 4 in IG).** He's a peer showing his screen, not a presenter.
- **Speaks from his own workflow.** "I still edit all my videos like end to end." / "I have an auto poster automation that I'm basically just like, hey, I just finished this video, posted all platforms."

## Content Structure Patterns

- **IG demo reel (2 of 5, about 150 to 250 words):** bold claim, then "let me show you exactly what this does", then First up / Next / "here's the part where it gets crazy", then a payoff line ("It's literally never been easier to blow up on social media"), then the comment keyword.
- **IG hype-check rant (1 of 5):** a question at the trend, then "at first I thought wow... [it's] the biggest waste of time", then he went to the source and built his own, then he teases the verdict without giving it, then the comment keyword for the full YT video.
- **IG tier list (1 of 5):** step, tier, one-line reason tied to his own usage. It has no CTA.
- **IG sponsored review (1 of 5):** a joke hook, favorite part, then "the bad news" done honestly, then the keyword for the link.
- **YT build or tutorial (all 3, 9 to 19 min):** hook and proof it's real (0 to 60s), then the "harsh reality" problem, then the promise ("By the end of this video, I promise you're going to have..."), then the early Skool skip-the-tutorial pitch, then "Let's get into it", then prerequisites or architecture ("So, basically..."), then a live run with honest reactions, then the verdict, then the Skool stack, then the sign-off.
- **Formats NOT in this batch:** story, case study, transparency or money talk, and anything filmed vertical-first for YT.

## Recurring Themes [current]

- **AI video editing autonomy (3 of 3 YT, 1 IG).** "One shot" comes up 14 times across 2 YT: one prompt, walk away, come back to a postable edit.
- **Claude over OpenAI.** Better edits for the same price, plus "Anthropic just gives me a much better vibe."
- **Separating AI slop from real leverage.** He tier-ranks where AI helps: research, thumbnails and posting are S tier, ideation and filming are C tier, scripting and editing are B tier.
- **Time saved and working while you sleep.** "Claude editing your videos while you sleep." / "Huge, huge time save."
- **Taste is the moat.** "Instead of relying on the AI to know how to edit, which it doesn't, we're going to use my human creativity... and lock it in." AI runs the presets, and a human sets the style.

## What NOT to Do (Anti-Patterns)

- Don't make a claim without a receipt on screen or a number. Every "it worked" gets shown live.
- Don't write IG reels with YT filler. IG is tight: no "you know", no "right?", minimal "like".
- Don't write YT like a polished script either. It runs on "So," / "Now," / "right?" and thinking out loud.
- Don't gush. At least one honest criticism or caveat goes in every review or demo.
- No "apply", DM-to-call or high-ticket CTAs. The IG CTA is a comment keyword for the video or resource. The YT CTA is the Skool, first link in the description.
- Don't carry transcription typos into scripts (ChatGBT, Clawed, school).
- No em dashes in script copy. Use a comma, a period or a colon.
