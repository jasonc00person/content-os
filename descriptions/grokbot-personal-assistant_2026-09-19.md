Summary ⤵️

I gave Grokbot a real phone number and turned it into my 24/7 personal assistant. It handles my tasks, my calendar, my Gmail, and all the phone calls I don't want to make. Most of the AI personal assistant builds on YouTube are super overcomplicated and take hours to set up. This one starts from a free template, and I build the whole thing live on camera so you can follow right along.

Here's how it works. You install the free Jarvis template into Grokbot (link above), then connect Bland, which is basically T-Mobile for AI agents: it gives your bot a phone number so it can call and text. You make a Bland API key, paste it into Grokbot's secure vault, and pick a voice. I cloned a custom Jarvis voice from a 14 second audio sample with Bland's instant clone, added Bland's official MCP, and had it call my phone as a test.

Then I connected Gmail and Google Calendar, set up a morning brief call, turned off the office background ambiance, made call recordings the default on outbound calls, and set up mid-call actions so the phone agent can actually do things while we're talking. On the test call I told it to put an event on my calendar, and it was on there before we hung up.

In the demo, the assistant calls me with my daily brief: what's on my calendar and which emails need my attention. On that same call I had it draft a reply to a brand deal email with my 10K minimum and move a calendar event, and both were done when I checked. Then I had it call my friend Andrew, check his availability against my calendar, lock in a gym session, and put it on my calendar. You hear the full recording of that call in the video.

I picked Grokbot and Bland for the simplicity. Normally I'm a big Claude Code guy, but Grokbot is made for the everyday person, and since it has an MCP with Bland you can build whatever you need just by chatting with it.
