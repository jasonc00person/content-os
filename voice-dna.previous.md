# Jason's Voice DNA

Voice rulebook for all script generation. Built from 42 top-performing videos in `voice-corpus/` (27 IG + 15 YT, ranked by views). Era tags: **[current]** = ai-automations niche (write this by default); **[old]** = viral-content era. When eras conflict, current wins; untagged patterns hold across both eras.

## Speech Patterns & Signature Phrases

### Openers

Hook = first spoken sentence on IG. On YT the **title** is the hook — the spoken open is a proof stack, not a one-liner. Numbers in hooks are always exact ($2,000/mo, $25.73, 49%, 30 minutes) — never rounded.

- **"I just built/replaced/sold X" [current — dominant, 7 of 27 IG]** — first-person completed action with a dollar/time figure in sentence one. "I just replaced my $2,000 a month content research team with one single N8N automation and it only took me 30 minutes to set up." (763K) / "I just sold this AI onboarding automation for 3k. And in this video, I'm leaking all the sauce." (91K)
- **"Are you still [outdated manual behavior]?"** — question hook attacking the viewer's workflow; recycled verbatim across years with updated receipts (10K → 100K → 170K followers). "Are you still manually posting from the Instagram app? I've gained over 10,000 followers in the past two months, and I have never posted from the Instagram app." (2.6M)
- **"It's time to fire/throw away [tool or person]"** — imperative replacement command, instantly justified. "It's time to throw away your microphone. You don't need it anymore, because it just got replaced by AI." (4.5M — corpus #1) / "It's time to fire your setter. I hate to say it, but they're cooked." (55K) [current]
- **Callout** — attack the viewer's behavior/content, pledge the fix in one breath. "This is how most of you guys use ChatGBT. Give me 10 content ideas about marketing. That's terrible. Let me teach you how to use ChatGBT properly in less than 30 seconds." (2.1M)
- **"I'm about to give away [$ asset] for free"** — secret-reveal; sentence two proves the price tag is real. "...yes, it is actually worth $500, because I actually sold it to Justin Ho, the viral genius, for $500 back in April." (831K)
- **Pain question** — hyper-specific frustration. "Have you ever struggled to find the perfect background music for your video?" (700K)
- **Sentence two is always a receipts beat** — personal numbers (his views, followers, sales), never generic stats. "And yeah, it works. I tried it on my last post, got over 200,000 views, tried it for my clients, got all these viral videos for them, so it works."
- **Line three: hype escalation or comic deflation.** Hype: "What you're about to witness is some next-level Black Mirror-type sh**." Deflation: "I really outdid myself on this one. What can I say? I mean, I'm go to... Anyways, check this out."
- **Early objection preempt (~15s in):** "Now, I know what you're thinking. Jason, you can't use third-party scheduling apps like Hootsuite or Sprout Social. You're right. That's why we're not using those."
- **Format rule:** tutorial-walkthrough opens second-person (question/callout → "Let me teach you..."); giveaway-demo opens first-person bold-claim → "Let me show you exactly what this entire automation does."
- **Frequency check:** bold-claim is the most common hook type (11 of 27), but the top 3 videos by views are ALL second-person hooks (microphone, manual posting, ChatGBT callout). "I just built" peaks at 763K. Don't default to first-person when a second-person attack is available.

### Filler / Transition Words

- **"just"** — the signature minimizer; lands before nearly every imperative and always inside the CTA. "Now just open up the Claude desktop app... Now just go back to Zernio..." Combo tic: "you basically just connect all your accounts."
- **"basically" (11/26)** heads explanation sentences: "It's basically reverse engineering the the algorithm." **"literally" (9/26)** before effort/time/result claims: "It took me literally five minutes."
- **"All right, so / Okay, so"** opens screen-share beats. **"Anyways"** recovers from a self-interrupt or bit. **"check this out / Check it out"** invites the demo.
- **"guys" (8/26)** at payoff reveals and CTA pivots: "And there we have it guys." **"Look,"** as attention reset: "Look, I'm sure you've seen all these fake gurus online."
- **"As you can see (here)" (5/26)** when cutting to screen proof: "As you can see here, last night it outreached about 20 ICP prospects."
- **"Now, I know what you're thinking. [objection in viewer's words]"** → blunt rebuttal: "Wrong. You're wrong. Just look at my account." / "No, you're wrong. Go look it up."
- **Demo pivot:** "So, here's how it works." / "Here is exactly how it works." — "exactly" appears in 10 of 26 transcripts; use it.
- **Numbered scaffolds:** "Step one... Step two..." / "Phase 1... Phase 2... Phase 3." for system breakdowns; unlabeled chains (First... Next... Then... Now...) for tutorials.
- **Mid-video re-hooks:** "But here's where it gets crazy." / "Now here's the kicker." / "and this is where the magic happens." / "But wait, there's more."
- **"Let me..." teach pivot (7/26):** "Let me teach you how to use ChatGBT properly in less than 30 seconds." / "Let me be real with you."
- **Second-person future chains (9/26):** "you're going to copy the link... then you're going to group them together"; live demos flip to "what we're going to do is..."
- **[current] spoken hedges:** "you know," mid-sentence, and approximator "like" before numbers/analogies: "waste like 10 hours a week" / "Kind of like the first suit that Tony Stark built in the cave."

### Closers

- **Master CTA (23 of 26 IG):** "[So] if you want X, just comment KEYWORD down below and I'll send Y." Always names the deliverable concretely; always the literal final beat, never mid-reel. "So if you want the whole thing, and a full guide on exactly how to use it, just comment system, and I'll send it over completely free."
- **"down below" [current]** — zero occurrences pre-Dec-2024, near-universal after. Old era said "I'll send it over to you."
- **[current] deliverables are code:** repo, JSON workflow, skill, template. "If you guys want the full repo for free, so you can copy and paste this system into your business just comment repo down below." Old era promised folders and Notion templates.
- **[current] destination = Skool:** "I'll send you a link to my school community where I have all my templates, automations, and all my resources inside there. I'll see you inside. Peace."
- **Sign-off stack [current]:** farewell line + "Peace." — "I'll see you in the next one. Peace." / "Hope to see you there." / "I hope you enjoyed the free sauce. Peace out." Old reels hard-stop on the CTA sentence; current ones always sign off.
- **Value qualifiers:** "completely free" — including the signature ungrammatical "I will send it to you for completely free" — and "I will send it to your DMs." / "I will sauce it straight to your DMs."
- **"sauce" (7/27, both eras):** "I'm leaking all the sauce." / "no one is giving away this level of sauce for free."
- Save/share CTAs exist but are rare (3/26, all old-era tutorials) — comment-keyword is the default.

### Energy Markers

- **Profanity as intensifier (8/26):** "I'm actually f***ing goaded at viral content marketing." / "I have optimized the f*** out of this thing." / "Like, I don't have to do shit."
- **Emphasis doubling:** "really, really important" / "I have never, ever posted from the Instagram app." / "Why? Why would you do that?"
- **Negation stacking for effortlessness:** "I don't even have to lift a finger" (verbatim in 2 entries) / "I didn't have to hire a designer. I didn't have to pay anyone, and I didn't have to wait for anyone."
- **Payoff exclamations after demo results:** "Boom. Easy peasy lemon squeezy." / "Just like that, now your video sounds like you're on a freaking Joe Rogan podcast." / "...and you're good to go."
- **Hook re-affirmation:** "That's right, AI just leveled up again." / "And yeah, it works."
- **[current] slang:** cooked, in the trenches, dialed, bangers, pleb, garbanzo beans, lock in, "ChadGBT / Chat GBT" pronunciation. Insult-affection: "even if you're dumb as f**k, like you will be able to understand this."

## Voice Characteristics

- **Persona [current]: cocky builder-inventor** — "my entire workflow that I invented with my own brain"; pop-culture analogies: "it's kind of like the Mark 6 suit that Tony wears at the beginning of Avengers." The "viral content marketer" identity is retired.
- **Receipts are personal and living** — follower count climbs across entries (10K → 100K → 140K → 170K). Always quote the CURRENT number, never an old one.
- **Honest-limits beat [current, every long demo]:** "This is not a replacement for your brain and your creative thinking." / "again, not a magic pill... treat it like an employee." Plus disclosure when relevant: "This is not sponsored. Okay, I am not getting paid to promote the software."
- **Viewer = business operator [current]:** "Especially if you're running a business as a content creator in 2026." / "Doesn't matter if you're a student, business owner, you work a nine to five job." Old era addressed creators chasing views.
- **IG vs YT register:** reels = one spoken sentence, go. YT = 1–2 min proof-stack intro ("I've made over $28,000 in a single week"), named self-intro ("My name is Jason..."), agenda beat ("First, we're going to cover... Then... And then finally..."), then the fixed pivot: "So without further ado, join me on the computer and let's get into this." YT keeps mistakes in on purpose — "I want you guys to see the raw process."
- **Tool stack [current]:** n8n, Claude by exact model ("content strategist agent running on Claude, Sonnet 4.5"), Claude Code/skills, Apify, Zernio, Poppy. Narrates systems as phases, nodes, agents, sub-agents, JSON. Name-drops AI-world figures ("The fix actually comes from the goat Andresh Karpathy"), not creator gurus.

## Content Structure Patterns

- **IG giveaway-demo (22 of 27 IG — the default):** (1) one-sentence bold-claim/dollar hook → (2) instant receipts in seconds 5–15 ("It's made me over $100,000 last year. And yes, it actually is worth $3,000 because as you can see here, I've sold this course for $3,000 to many, many people") → (3) optional objection preempt → (4) screen demo dead-center, narrated as numbered phases/steps → (5) comment-keyword CTA + sign-off as the literal last lines. 72–132s current era.
- **IG tutorial-walkthrough (5 of 27):** callout/question hook → personal-numbers authority → objection preempt → imperative numbered steps with the pivotal one flagged ("Step three, and this is where the magic happens.") → quantified payoff ("Now you can plan out weeks or months of content in advance in just 30 minutes."). 40–94s.
- **YT tutorial-walkthrough (11 of 15 YT):** proof-stack intro → agenda → "join me on the computer" → demo body (9–57 min) → link CTAs BOTH mid-roll ("But enough of that subtle plug promo. Let's get into actually the process") and at the end → "I will see you guys in the next one. Peace out."
- **Variants that exist:** storytime giveaway (hook → price-proof → origin/theft story → scarcity → CTA), build-in-public (show finished artifacts FIRST — "it costs literally $25.73 per month to replace a full-time content researcher" — then run the machine live, then sell the shortcut), case-study interview (cold-open client results clip → intro → before/skepticism/results/"what was the secret sauce" arc → single Skool CTA), raw-talk transparency (money number in the first breath, no demo, no CTA, motivational close). There is NO rant-take format anywhere in the corpus — don't invent one.
- **Winners are reusable assets:** proven scripts get re-shot near-verbatim years later with refreshed proof numbers and new jokes ($500-system reel word-for-word 2024 → 2026; posting-scheduler reel three times). Reels funnel to the matching YT long-form: "I posted a YouTube video about it. So go and watch that."

## Recurring Themes [current era]

1. **AI replaces humans:** "It's time to fire your setter. I hate to say it, but they're cooked." Recurring softener: "I hate to say it, but it's basically replacing the job of content researchers."
2. **Runs while I sleep / autopilot:** "it runs every Sunday in my sleep, and it only costs 90 cents per run" / "all of this runs completely on autopilot. Like, I don't have to do shit. I literally just sit around and this just happens."
3. **Data, not guessing:** "We're basically just reverse engineering the algorithm to get a data proven content strategy." / "So instead of guessing what to post, you actually know for a fact that this is what your audience wants to see."
4. **ROI math, two flavors:** replaced-cost ("I just replaced my $30 thumbnail editor with ChadGBT in five minutes") and time-saved ("This automation saves me 20 plus hours every week and it writes my content for me").
5. **Everything funnels to the Skool community** — free breakdown via comment keyword; the full files, code, and templates live inside Skool.
6. **Client proof:** Albert (1K → 20K followers, $200 → $3,600 MRR) and his own post-level receipts ("my original post about this, which by the way got 30,000 f***ing comments").

## What NOT to Do (Anti-Patterns)

- **Don't pepper scripts with "Right?"** — it appears in exactly 1 of 26 transcripts. (The old voice-dna called it a signature tic; the corpus says no.) His real confirmation move is hook re-affirmation: "That's right, AI just leveled up again."
- **No "Pooch"/"Pooosh" sign-offs** — zero corpus occurrences. It's "Peace." / "Peace out."
- **Dead CTAs:** no apply / DM-to-call / DFY-install offers ("comment viral and I will send you a link to speak with me directly" — retired 2026-06-09). No "smash the follow for more free value."
- **Retired vocabulary [old]:** steal-and-twist playbook ("build a creator list... start stealing"), fake-guru rant + free-course funnel ("flex their Lambos, flex their Rolexes"), brand-asset talk ("brand fonts... brand colors... brand transitions"), creator-guru name-drops (Hormozi/Eman/MrBeast as the peer set).
- **Never round the numbers** — "$25.73 per month," "49%," "got over 200,000 views." Vague stats are not his voice.
- **Never corporate, hedged, or soft-sold** — no LinkedIn polish, no buzzwords, no caveats beyond the deliberate not-sponsored/honest-limits beats.
- **Don't quote stale receipts** — an old follower or revenue number in a new script is an instant tell. Pull the current figure.
