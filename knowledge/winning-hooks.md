# Winning Hooks

Two layers: Jason's own proven openers (from `voice-corpus/` via `voice-dna.md`) and the market shapes winning right now (from `research/`). `hook-generator` owns the 8-structure taxonomy in `.claude/skills/hook-generator/reference/structures.md`; this page is the proof-linked shape list it checks against.

## Ground rules

- Short-form hook = the first spoken sentence. Long-form hook = the YouTube title paired with the thumbnail; the spoken open is a proof stack. Never substitute one for the other.
- Numbers in hooks are exact, never rounded. Sentence two is always a personal receipts beat.
- Bold-claim is the most common hook in the corpus, but the top three videos by views are all second-person attacks. Don't default to first-person when a second-person attack is available.
- Don't jam the CTA into the hook. The Skool ask comes later.

Source: `voice-dna.md`

## Jason's proven openers (reuse and re-shoot)

- **"I just built / replaced / sold X"** with a dollar or time figure in sentence one. "I just replaced my $2,000 a month content research team with one single N8N automation and it only took me 30 minutes to set up." (763K plays, `voice-corpus/ig-DLlLmRPgX96.md`)
- **"Are you still [outdated manual behavior]?"** Attacks the viewer's workflow; re-shot verbatim across years with fresh receipts. (2.6M plays, `voice-corpus/ig-C4YY12SxuRo.md`)
- **"It's time to fire / throw away [tool or person]."** Imperative replacement, justified in the next breath. "It's time to throw away your microphone. You don't need it anymore, because it just got replaced by AI." (4.5M plays, corpus #1, `voice-corpus/ig-C6qt2B4NKA1.md`)
- **Callout**: attack the viewer's behavior, pledge the fix in one breath. "This is how most of you guys use ChatGBT... That's terrible. Let me teach you how to use ChatGBT properly in less than 30 seconds." (2.1M plays, `voice-corpus/ig-C6dzmuxB3dE.md`)
- **"I'm about to give away [$ asset] for free"**, with sentence two proving the price tag is real. (831K plays, `voice-corpus/ig-C9hk80xPxql.md`)
- **Pain question**, hyper-specific frustration. "Have you ever struggled to find the perfect background music for your video?" (700K plays, `voice-corpus/ig-C7eWHBSPCwE.md`)
- **Line three** is hype escalation ("some next-level Black Mirror-type sh**") or comic deflation ("I really outdid myself on this one. What can I say?").

Source: `voice-dna.md`, `voice-corpus/index.md`

## Market shapes winning now (summer 2026)

Instagram:
- **Outcome-first number + comment-gated freebie.** "ChatGPT applied to 500 jobs for me" (41x breakout, 27K comments). The comment-gate CTA is on every winning AI reel. `research/IG-Competitor-Research_2026-06-16_211310.html`, `research/IG-Feed-Research_2026-07-12.html`
- **"X is dead / X killed Y" fear-FOMO** (N8N is dead, Google killed SEO, Claude killed finance) plus "leaked / free" bait. `research/IG-Competitor-Research_2026-06-16_211310.html`
- **"Most [role] waste hours doing manually what these 3 [tools] do in one click"** listicle + save CTA (23x, 5K comments). `research/IG-Feed-Research_2026-07-12.html`
- **Build-your-own-Jarvis wish fulfillment** in N steps (11.7x carousel, 11K comments). `research/IG-Competitor-Research_2026-06-16_211310.html`
- **Insider meme roast** ("These [tool] users need to be stopped", >100x). `research/IG-Feed-Research_2026-07-12.html`

YouTube titles:
- **"I turned X into Y"** transformation promise backed by a live-dashboard thumb (277K, 4.9x). `research/YT-Competitor-Research_2026-06-18_012507.html`
- **News-shock + permanence**: "just changed X forever", "changes EVERYTHING", model name + INSANE. Same-day launch reactions were the biggest breakouts. `research/YT-Competitor-Research_2026-06-18_012507.html`
- **Income claim + negative warning**: "I asked Claude Code to make me as much money as possible" with "This should be illegal" on the thumb (121K, 3.1x). `research/YT-Competitor-Research_2026-07-02_115514.html`
- **Listicle + loss warning**: "5 Use Cases You Must Do NOW (Or Lose Thousands in 1 Week)" (112K, 2.8x). `research/YT-Competitor-Research_2026-07-02_115514.html`
- **Reassurance**: "You're Not Behind (Yet)" (2.3x). Fits the Skool-facing beginner exactly. `research/YT-Feed-Research_2026-07-24.html`
- **Deadline framing**: "Do This Before 2027" (20.7x on a small channel). `research/YT-Feed-Research_2026-07-24.html`
- **Compression promise**: "1000+ hours of Learning Claude in 15 Minutes" (5.8x); "Give me 26 minutes, I'll give you 10,000 hours" (2.7x). `research/YT-Feed-Research_2026-07-27.html`, `research/YT-Feed-Research_2026-07-24.html`
- **"You're Using X Wrong (20 Things It Can Actually Do)"** capability listicle. `research/YT-Feed-Research_2026-07-27.html`
- **Cheap-price shock**: "For Just $1" bolted to a premium outcome; **cost-as-credibility**: "I Spent $400 Benching Opus-5". `research/YT-Feed-Research_2026-07-24.html`

Thumbnails: screenshotted proof (dashboard, table, tweet) beats staged reaction shots; alternative is a caps gut-punch ("NOTHING HERE IS REAL", "FREE FOREVER"); us-vs-them race visuals for tool verdict videos. `research/YT-Competitor-Research_2026-07-02_115514.html`, `research/YT-Competitor-Research_2026-06-18_012507.html`

## Jason-specific hook rules

- Lead with the spoken line; text overlay supports it.
- Make the viewer feel behind if they still do the manual workflow, then show the live system fast.
- The receipts beat uses current numbers from the proof bank in `backbone/messaging.md`, verified the day you write.
- CTA lives at the end: comment keyword → resource → the Skool community (name/price per `backbone/offer.md`). No apply or DM-to-call language.
