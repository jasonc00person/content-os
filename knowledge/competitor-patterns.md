# Competitor Patterns

Tracked handles live in `competitor-list.md`. Two research lanes feed this page: tracked-handle scans (`*-Competitor-Research`) and algorithm scans of the logged-in feeds (`*-Feed-Research`).

## Instagram

- **Comment-gate is universal.** On all 15 competitor picks and every winning AI reel in the feed scan; it inflates comments into the thousands and is the engagement flywheel of the niche. `research/IG-Competitor-Research_2026-06-16_211310.html`, `research/IG-Feed-Research_2026-07-12.html`
- **Claude is the recurring subject** (Fable 5, Claude Code, Cowork, "build your own Jarvis / agentic OS"). Hooks skew fear-FOMO ("X is dead", "killed the industry"). `research/IG-Competitor-Research_2026-06-16_211310.html`
- **Talking-head reels with a delayed reveal beat carousels**, but Chase's 5-step "build your Fable 5 OS" carousel still hit 11.7x with one clear step per slide and a swipeable Jarvis fantasy. mavgpt's rapid "bad / good / great" side-by-side comparison (3.1x) always lands on Claude. `research/IG-Competitor-Research_2026-06-16_211310.html`
- **Builder-culture memes outperform tutorials** in the feed: vibecoderian's roast of vibe-coded apps (>100x, 10K reshares) and realchasko's "feeling of building" post. Tool brands (Higgsfield) run pure comment-gate product drops. `research/IG-Feed-Research_2026-07-12.html`

## YouTube (tracked channels)

- **Claude IS the entire pool.** All 25 picks in the June scan were Claude Code workflows or model-drop reactions; nothing outside the Anthropic news cycle cracked any channel's top 5. `research/YT-Competitor-Research_2026-06-18_012507.html`
- **Title archetypes:** news-shock + permanence, "I turned X into Y", income claim + negative warning (nateherk "This should be illegal", 121K, 3.1x), listicle + loss warning (Chase, 112K, 2.8x), us-vs-them verdicts (Claude vs Figma race thumb, 87K). `research/YT-Competitor-Research_2026-06-18_012507.html`, `research/YT-Competitor-Research_2026-07-02_115514.html`
- **Thumbnails close the click with proof**: real dashboards, benchmark tables, "official" tweets, EUR payout readouts. Half the July pool did this. Fallback is a caps gut-punch. `research/YT-Competitor-Research_2026-07-02_115514.html`
- **Speed on the drop beats production polish**: same-day launch reactions were the biggest relative breakouts (approx 8x median on small channels). `research/YT-Competitor-Research_2026-06-18_012507.html`
- **Every competitor description funnels to a Skool** (free group + paid tier + "work with me"). The motion is the same as ours; the differentiator has to be the deployed system on screen. `research/YT-Competitor-Research_2026-07-02_115514.html`

## Long-form structure lessons (from `transcripts/analysis/`)

- **Proof-first cold open**: one sentence, then the finished artifact plays; promise at 0:16; render waits become CTA slots; manual checkpoints graduate into a full-autonomy handoff; cost kicker held for the end. `transcripts/analysis/i-made-vox-style-motion-graphics-claude-code-omni_structure_2026-07-13.md`
- **Tier-list escalating levels** with one mental model reused to score every level, pain-triggered transitions, and an honest down-rank that builds trust. `transcripts/analysis/every-claude-code-memory-system-compared_2026-06-15_STRUCTURE.md`
- **Framework + over-the-shoulder demo pegged to a news event**, demos delayed and escalated, honest corrections on purpose. `transcripts/analysis/i-turned-claude-fable-into-the-ultimate-second-brain_2026-06-15_STRUCTURE.md`
- **Tool breakdown with nested curiosity loops** (macro: does it deliver; micro: a skeptical claim resolved by a live test). `transcripts/analysis/this-open-source-repo-just-solved-claude-codes-1-problem_2026-06-15_STRUCTURE.md`

## Steal

Comment-gate CTA mechanics, outcome-first hooks, screenshotted proof in thumbs, same-day model-launch reactions, "for normal people" framing, proof-first cold opens, render-wait CTA slots, honest-limits and down-rank beats, build-in-public documentary energy.

## Don't steal

Dev-only posture, prompt-collector listicles with no running system, clip-farm money math, generic "learn Claude" packaging without a creator outcome, "apply / work with me" backend theater (our one CTA is the Skool community, name/price per `backbone/offer.md`).
