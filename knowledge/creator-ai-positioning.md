# Positioning (current backbone generation)

Compiled from `backbone/` as of 2026-08-03 (`vision.md`) and 2026-07-13 (`offer.md`, `icp.md`, `messaging.md`). Filename is fixed because `scriptwriter` reads it by name; the lane inside is whatever the backbone says this generation.

## The lane

**Leverage AI to build a viral brand and scale a business with content, even starting from zero.** AI is the mechanism; a viral brand and a scaled business are the outcomes. This is the hybrid lane Jason locked 2026-07-13: it merges the May "viral content" lane and the June "AI automations" lane. Content is the lead use case, automations are the multiplier.

Source: `backbone/offer.md`, `backbone/icp.md`

The contrast to keep drawing:

- Not AI content slop, not "real creators do it manually."
- Not developer tutorials. Translate every build for creators, coaches, agencies.
- Not high-ticket theory. Anti-LARP, anti-gatekeep: real, deployed systems taught by an operator who runs them daily on his own brand.
- Not "go viral" hype with nothing underneath. Virality is a system output.

Source: `backbone/vision.md`, `backbone/messaging.md`

## The offer

One offer, one motion. **AI Leverage Lab**, the Skool community (name/price per `backbone/offer.md`). Every content piece and every CTA points at joining it. YouTube is the signup engine; topic choice on the channel is a revenue decision.

Deliverables in live-page order (flagship first): Claude Video Editor (plug-n-play editing files), Viral AI Content System, Claude Code For Dummies, AI Automation Vault. Bonuses: twice-weekly mastermind calls with direct feedback on the member's content, plus the network. Guarantee: unlimited tech support, deliberately no outcome guarantee.

A paid scope-call side door for done-for-you work exists on the about page only (`backbone/offer.md`). It never gets its own funnel and never appears in content. Retired and never pitched: the old high-ticket install and the older membership tiers; no "apply" or DM-to-call CTAs anywhere.

Source: `backbone/offer.md`, `backbone/vision.md`

## Who it's for

Creators, coaches, and agencies scaling with content. Posting or running the business manually, no systems, time-poor, drowning in AI noise, burned by expensive theory programs. They want views, a brand, hours back, early-mover status, and monetized attention.

Not for: free-hack tourists, magic-button seekers, non-builders who want it done for them without the scope call.

Source: `backbone/icp.md`

## The funnel

**Demo → YouTube education → Skool.** An IG post demos a real system running (output and speed, never a claim), a YouTube video teaches it, the Skool sells the shortcut. Rules: the demo IS the ad; IG never teaches the full build; each demo names the hours replaced; CTA chain = comment keyword → YT deep-dive → Skool join.

Source: `backbone/messaging.md`

## Belief shifts (every piece reinforces at least one)

1. Leverage is a system, not luck, talent, or grind. Virality included.
2. Real education shouldn't cost five figures. Working systems at a community price is the counter-position.
3. AI is the leverage right now. Refusing it is why people stay stuck at low views and long weeks.
4. Content IS the business. For coaches and agencies it's the client-getter.
5. Free content can't fix you. You need the systems, a community, and someone critiquing YOUR work.
