## Avoid

- **Finance and money-system topics.** Off-lane for this audience: the feed skill permanently blocks finance and news channels and the IG scan flags tax-strategy follows as drift (`research/YT-Feed-Research_2026-07-12.html`, `research/IG-Feed-Research_2026-07-12.html`). Session memory records the July Claude bookkeeping video underperforming while the editor video sold (`/Users/jasoncooperson/.claude/projects/-Users-jasoncooperson-Projects-content-os/memory/project_next_yt_video_finance.md`). Don't pitch it again.
- **Developer-only Claude Code content.** Theo's "everything we knew about software has changed" (20.4x) only works as "what this means if you're not a developer" (`research/YT-Feed-Research_2026-07-12.html`).
- **Clip-farm and faceless-channel money math.** LARP-adjacent; steal only the concrete "X × Y = $Z/day" format for real systems (`research/IG-Feed-Research_2026-07-12.html`).
- **Hook or personal-branding content with no AI layer.** Flat numbers even from Mino (`research/YT-Feed-Research_2026-07-24.html`).
- **Tool hype without a running demo.** The demo IS the ad; each demo names the hours replaced (`backbone/messaging.md`).
